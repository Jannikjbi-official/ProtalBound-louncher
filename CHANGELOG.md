# Changelog

All notable changes to the Portalbound Launcher. The launcher shows the section of the installed version under **What's new**, and every GitHub release uses it as its release notes.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-24 - The Portal Opens

### New
- **One click to play** – the launcher installs Minecraft 26.2 and Java 25 for you and connects you straight to `play.portalbound.xyz`.
- **Release countdown** – the play button stays locked until the portal opens on January 1, 2027 at 1:00 PM (German time) and unlocks automatically, no update needed.
- **Microsoft sign-in** with multiple accounts and your skin right on the start page.
- **News** – updates, events and sneak peeks from the team, straight in the launcher.
- **Automatic updates** – new launcher versions download in the background and install with one click.
- **What's new** – after every update you see what changed.

### Portalbound look
- Violet and cyan portal design with the Portalbound logo, fonts and artwork from portalbound.xyz.
- Quick links to the website, the store and the Discord.

### Under the hood
- Based on Helios Launcher by Daniel Scalzi, rebuilt for Portalbound and vanilla Minecraft.
